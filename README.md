## Executive Summary
This project provides a comprehensive, unified view of Netflix's content performance by integrating three distinct data sources: the content catalog, audience ratings (IMDb), and financial box office revenue. The primary objective is to empower stakeholders with actionable, data-driven insights to guide content production and acquisition strategies, ultimately maximizing Return on Investment (ROI) and enhancing subscriber retention.

---

## Business Problem and Opportunity

* The Challenge: Streaming platforms frequently suffer from data silos. Data regarding "what is available" (catalog) is often separated from "how the audience reacts" (ratings) and "how much it costs/earns" (revenue).
* The Opportunity: Establishing a Single Source of Truth that connects content quality, audience preferences, and financial performance. This enables decision-makers to strategically allocate production budgets toward the most profitable and highly engaged content categories.

---

## Key Business Insights

1. The Rating vs. Revenue Equation: High IMDb ratings do not automatically guarantee top-tier box office revenue. However, content that achieves the "Golden Ratio" (high ratings combined with a massive volume of user votes) ensures stable financial performance with significantly lower risk.
2. ROI by Genre: Documentary features and Stand-up Comedy specials yield the highest Return on Investment. This is primarily due to their exceptionally low production costs compared to Action and Sci-Fi titles, which require massive budgets to generate high gross revenue.
3. Regional Content Impact: Locally produced content in specific international markets (e.g., South Korea, Spain) demonstrates engagement rates well above the global average, presenting a highly cost-effective expansion opportunity.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

* Budget Allocation: Redirect a strategic percentage of funds from high-budget, high-risk blockbusters toward acquiring or producing international original content. This category has proven to attract new demographics at a highly competitive cost.
* Recommendation Engine Optimization: Adjust the platform's algorithm to prioritize content possessing the "Golden Ratio" (High Rating + High Popularity) on user homepages, directly reducing subscriber churn rates.

---

## Project Deliverables and Tech Stack

The final deliverable is an interactive dashboard that mirrors the native Netflix User Interface and User Experience (UI/UX), featuring a custom-built, horizontally scrollable poster gallery.

Technologies utilized to ensure data integrity and visual excellence:
* Python (Pandas): Utilized for data extraction, extensive cleaning, handling missing values, and executing the ETL pipeline (including resolving special character conflicts in official movie titles for accurate data merging).
* Power Query: Applied for data modeling, resolving multi-valued attributes (e.g., splitting comma-separated cast and genres), and constructing a robust Star Schema.
* Power BI and DAX: Used for computing Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and designing the interactive reporting interface.
* HTML, CSS, and JavaScript: Implemented via custom visuals to bypass native visualization limitations, creating a dynamic, responsive horizontal scroll component for movie posters.
