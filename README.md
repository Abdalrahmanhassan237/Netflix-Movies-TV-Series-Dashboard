##  The Bottom Line
This project transforms raw Netflix data (catalog size, IMDb ratings, and box office revenue) into a single, interactive dashboard. It answers the most critical question for content platforms: **What should we produce or buy next to maximize profit margins and keep subscribers paying?**

##  The Business Problem
Decision-makers often look at content and financial data separately. This creates a blind spot. A movie might have great ratings but lose money, or generate massive revenue but cost too much to make. This dashboard merges these datasets to reveal the *true* Return on Investment (ROI) of every genre and market.

---

##  What The Data Tells Us (Key Findings)

* **The Profit Illusion:** Action and Adventure movies generate massive cash, but their massive budgets destroy profit margins. Conversely, **Comedies, Sci-Fi, and Thrillers deliver up to a 200% ROI** due to their low production costs.
* **The Retention Secret:** Movies attract new users (forming 70% of the catalog), but TV shows keep them subscribed. Interestingly, the most reliable retention drivers are not Netflix Originals, but licensed legacy shows (like *Grey's Anatomy*, running for 15+ seasons).
* **The "Fail Fast" Reality:** Netflix is ruthless with its Original series. The average show is canceled after just **1.76 seasons** if it doesn't become an instant hit.
* **Growth is Moving East:** The US market is saturated. India is now the 2nd largest content producer on the platform, marking a massive strategic shift toward capturing Asian audiences.

---

##  Action Plan (Strategic Recommendations)

1. **Maximize Profit Margins:** Shift a portion of the high-risk "Action" movie budgets into highly efficient, high-ROI genres (Thrillers and Comedies) to secure actual net profit.
2. **Lock Down Legacy Licenses:** Aggressively defend the broadcasting rights for classic network TV shows. They act as "comfort viewing" and are the ultimate safety net against subscriber churn.
3. **Double Down on Asia:** Increase local production investments in markets like India and South Korea. They offer lower baseline production costs and target populations with the highest streaming growth potential.
4. **Rethink Cancellations:** Instead of abruptly canceling shows, create low-budget "wrap-up" movies. This keeps the existing fanbase happy and maintains the show's replay value in the catalog.

## 🛠️Under The Hood (Tech Stack)

* **Data Engineering (Python / Pandas):** Extracted, cleaned, and merged disparate datasets, handling missing values and resolving complex text inconsistencies for accurate joining.
* **Data Modeling (Power Query):** Resolved multi-valued attributes (e.g., splitting comma-separated genres) and built a robust Star Schema.
* **Visualization (Power BI & DAX):** Designed interactive KPIs, financial tracking metrics, and dynamic filtering.
* **Custom UI (HTML/CSS/JS):** Coded a custom horizontal scrollable poster gallery to perfectly mimic the actual Netflix platform experience.
